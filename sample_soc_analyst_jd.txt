SOC Analyst L1 (Entry Level) - Bangalore, India

About the role:
We are looking for an entry-level SOC Analyst to join our 24x7 Security Operations Center. You will be the first line of defense, monitoring security alerts, performing initial triage, and escalating confirmed incidents to L2 analysts.

Responsibilities:
- Monitor security alerts generated by our SIEM platform (Splunk) around the clock
- Perform initial investigation and triage of security incidents
- Document incident details and actions taken in our ticketing system
- Escalate confirmed incidents to Level 2 analysts with clear documentation
- Analyze network traffic using Wireshark to identify suspicious activity
- Assist with phishing email analysis and malware triage
- Support vulnerability assessment activities as needed
- Participate in ongoing security training and skill development

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field
- Basic understanding of networking protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP)
- Familiarity with SIEM tools (Splunk, QRadar, or similar) is a strong plus
- Understanding of firewalls, IDS/IPS concepts
- Knowledge of Linux and Windows operating systems
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Good written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to work rotational shifts in a 24x7 environment
- CompTIA Security+ certification preferred but not mandatory for freshers
- Scripting knowledge (Python) is a plus for automation tasks

We welcome freshers who demonstrate strong foundational knowledge and a genuine willingness to learn, even without prior industry experience.
